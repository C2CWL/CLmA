## CLmA 4.3

- 修复工具轮次后自动压缩仍计入压缩前用量的问题。
- 修复大附件整份塞进提示词导致模型读不完的问题。
- 限制大附件内联预览的长度。
- 保留附件提示词中的原始文件路径。
- 达到字符上限后停止文档解析。
- 避免把半截抽取结果写入缓存。
- 避免复制大纯文本到抽取缓存。

## Changes
- Ignore pre-compaction usage after a tool round during automatic compaction.
- Stop inlining large attachments in full into prompts.
- Limit the size of inlined attachment previews.
- Keep the original file path in attachment prompts.
- Stop document parsing after reaching the character limit.
- Avoid caching partial text extraction results.
- Avoid copying large plain-text files into the extracted cache.
